04 / READ THIS BEFORE BELIEVING A DELTA

Statistical discipline.

Small score changes can be sampling noise. We compare configurations on the same sessions with paired resampling.

WHY PAIRED?

Shared sessions share variance.

An unpaired estimate gives roughly ±0.019 at n=200. A paired bootstrap resamples the same session indices for both configurations, directly measuring whether A beats B.

07 / STATED HONESTLY, NOT BURIED

Limitations.

01

Simulator-shaped language

Regex and exact evidence benefit from fixed templates and verbatim catalog metadata; natural paraphrases remain the largest robustness gap.

02

Freshness optimizes the metric

Rotating every shown product helps cumulative Hit@10, but a real storefront should retain strong matches and rotate only the tail.

03

Generic clarification

The scored Agent always asks “other.” A richer QuestionPolicy exists but is not wired into production response behavior.

04

No negative item feedback

The interface cannot learn “none of these” or why a specific recommendation failed.

05

No abstention

The Agent always returns ten products. Confidence is read-only and currently too flat to support a reliable abstain decision.

06

Hit@10 is cumulative

The verified 1.000 is within ten conversational turns, not single-shot Recall@10.
